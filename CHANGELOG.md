# Changelog

All notable changes to the voice-wayfinder plugin. Versions follow semantic versioning; the bridge/mod message format has its own `v` (see `plugin/types/index.d.ts`).

## Unreleased

- The voice pane is restyled. The status leads with an animated icon on the desktop (sound bars while Claude speaks or you speak, a pulse while listening, a spinner while connecting) beside a larger label, with the session's start time and cost on the right. Chips under it name the models in use: Speaks (the realtime model and its voice), Hears (transcription) and Understands (the answer check); the terminal shows them on one line. The question card gets a header, Resume and Unmute become the primary button when they are the thing to press, the current question in the list is highlighted and answered ones are dimmed, section titles are small and quiet, and the cost totals move to the foot. The bridge's state carries `models` (from the config) for this.
- Requirements can be talked through, not only answered yes or no. Claude now picks per question: quick confirmations still go in a round, but open questions (how something should work, edge cases, an answer that came with a condition) are discussed with `voice_converse` and the new `discuss: true`. You explain as long as you need and pause to think; the reply goes to Claude as said (no quick answer check, no "ได้ยินไม่ชัด"), and Claude says back what it understood and follows up until it can state the decision, then confirms it. A discussion reply ends after 1.2 s of quiet (`conversation.discussEndOfReplyMs`).
- A reply that stops mid-thought ("คิดว่าใช้ได้ แต่ว่า", "ถ้าเกิดว่า", "คือ", "เอ่อ…") is waited for 3.5 s (`conversation.unfinishedReplyMs`), so a thinking pause no longer ends your turn; a reply that sounds finished ends as quickly as before. OpenAI's semantic turn detection was tried for this and rejected: on Thai it split replies more than a plain pause, even mid-word ("ใช้ได้ ‖ ค"), and ended a short "ใช้ได้ค่ะ" 1.5–3.4 s late.
- Your speech is transcribed by `gpt-live-transcribe` (was `gpt-4o-transcribe`, which OpenAI shuts down on 26 Feb 2027). Your words now appear in the pane while you speak, not after each pause, and the final text arrives sooner (0.3–0.5 s after you stop, was 0.4–0.8 s). English terms come out as written ("main profile", "Assistant", "serial" instead of "เมนโปรไฟล์", "แอสซิสแทนต์", "ซีเรียล"): each question sends the English terms of what Claude says and of its card as recognition hints, ahead of the configured `vocabulary`. `gpt-transcribe` and `gpt-live-transcribe` get `languages` instead of `language`. Transcription costs $0.017 a minute (was $0.006).
- Fixed: long spoken answers got "ขอโทษค่ะ ได้ยินไม่ชัด" again and again. Two causes. Breath pauses of half a second split a long answer into short pieces, each transcribed without the rest, which came out as random words; speech is now split only after 1 s of quiet (`turnDetection.silenceDurationMs` 1000, was 500) and the reply is taken 0.4 s after its last piece (`endOfReplyMs` 400, was 900), so the wait after you finish stays about the same. And one garbled piece made the whole answer read as noise; a reply of 50 letters or more is now never taken for noise but goes to Claude as heard. A misheard "ทวนคำถาม…" ("หวานคำถามขอห้ามหน่อย", "บนคำถามข้อห้าใหม่") repeats the question. "ใครได้" and "ให้ได้" opening a reply to "…ใช้ได้ไหมคะ" read as "ใช้ได้". Short noise words that Luna splits between yes and no ("เฮ้ย") are asked again instead of taken as yes.
- Fixed: "ใช้ได้ แต่ว่า…" (yes with a condition) got "ขอโทษค่ะ ได้ยินไม่ชัด" again and again. Luna splits such a reply between yes and another answer, so neither kind reached 0.4 confidence; a reply now counts as noise only when the answer kinds together are under 0.6 likely too (real answers 0.64–0.94 live, noise 0.21–0.53). Replies opening with "ใช้ได้", "ใช่ได้", "คิดว่า…" or "บอกว่า…" count as a plain yes or no, and "เชื่อได้" (how recognition hears "ใช้ได้") reads as "ใช้ได้" when that is what was asked. A yes with "แต่…" or "แค่…" is acknowledged plainly instead of "ใช่ค่ะ", and its words are kept as the answer, so reading it back gives the condition too.
- The voice pane lists the round's questions (▶ asking, ✓ answered with the answer taken, ○ not yet, ⤼ skipped), and each one is a button. Click a question not yet answered and the round goes there, then comes back to the first one still open. Click an answered one and it is read back ("ข้อหนึ่ง ตอบไว้ว่า ใช่ ค่ะ จะเปลี่ยนไหมคะ"): keep it ("เหมือนเดิม", "ไม่"), change it ("เปลี่ยน": the question is asked again), or give the new answer straight away ("เอาข้อสองแทน"). Changed answers reach Claude marked with what they were. After a round, clicking a question asks Claude to go back to it (beside its next tool result, as a turn if it is idle, or in place of the question it was waiting on). New `voice_control` action `goto` (with `question`); the state carries `round` and `revisitDue`.
- Misheard answers can be fixed on the spot. When the confirmation was wrong ("ไม่ใช่นะคะ" after you said ใช่) and you say so ("เดี๋ยว เมื่อกี้บอกว่าใช่", "ฟังผิด", "ย้อนกลับไปข้อที่แล้ว", or a quick "ไม่ใช่ๆ" over the start of the next question), the bridge says "เมื่อกี้น่าจะฟังผิดค่ะ งั้นเรากลับไปข้อก่อนหน้าอีกครั้งนะคะ" (a ready clip), drops the misheard answer and asks that question again, then carries on (up to 3 times a round). The check sees the previous question and how its answer was taken; words that point back, and a "no" spoken in the first 1.5 s of the new question, count as a correction. Outside a round, Claude is told and asks again itself. A reply that opens with a plain yes or no ("ไม่ได้ ต้องใช้เบอร์โทรด้วย") is no longer mistaken for noise.
- Replies are understood, not just heard. Before a round moves on, OpenAI's Decisions API (`gpt-6-luna`, about 0.3 s and $0.00004 a reply; `openai.understanding`) reads each spoken reply against its question and card and returns its kind: yes, no, a numbered choice, another answer, a request to repeat, a question back, another subject, or noise. Noise (a flush, a cough, someone else talking, or any answer below 0.4 confidence) gets an instant ready clip, "ขอโทษค่ะ ได้ยินไม่ชัด พูดอีกทีได้ไหมคะ", and the reply is listened for again without reading the question again (twice at most). An answer is confirmed by a ready clip of what was understood ("ใช่ค่ะ", "ไม่ใช่นะคะ", "ข้อสองนะคะ") instead of a stock "ค่ะ". A request to repeat repeats the question; a question back or another subject stops the round and goes to Claude. Results tell Claude how each reply was understood. A lone "ไทย"/"ไฮ"/"ไฉ่" (how recognition hears "ใช่") reads as yes. Typed answers are taken as written. If the check fails or times out (2 s), the reply stands as before. `"api": "responses"` with a text model such as `gpt-4.1-mini` is the alternative (slower, writes its own confirmations). Its cost shows as "understanding" in the session summary.
- Fixed: back from a long pause while a question was being asked, the question waited for a reply instead of returning to Claude for the recap.
- Fixed: an answer like "ใช่ ไม่ต้องถามใหม่" ("yes, don't ask again") or a complaint like "ทำไมถามซ้ำ" was taken as a request to hear the question again, so the same question came back up to three times. Utterances that say not to repeat, or that hold an answer (ใช่, ได้, โอเค, a choice number), now count as answers. The log notes what was heard when a question is repeated.
- Pause button in the voice pane, for when someone comes to talk to you: the mic goes off, Claude stops mid-sentence, a waiting question stops counting toward its timeout, and Claude's next voice call waits. ▶ Resume picks up where it stopped (the interrupted line is said again from the start). Typing an answer while paused still counts. New `voice_control` actions `pause` and `resume`, and the activity `paused`.
- Back from a pause of a minute or more (`conversation.recapAfterPauseSec`, default 60), Claude first gives a short spoken recap of where things stand: a waiting voice call returns `resumed` with the pause's length, and when no voice call waits, the pane asks for the recap beside Claude's next tool result, or starts a turn if Claude is idle.
- Windows support (Windows 10 and 11). A new audio helper, `plugin/native/vw-audio/main.cpp`, speaks the same framed protocol as the Swift one: WASAPI playback, and the mic through the Windows Voice Capture DSP (echo cancellation plus noise suppression) with `--voice-processing`, or raw WASAPI without it. It builds with MSVC from Visual Studio 2022 Build Tools, found through `vswhere`.
- The bridge is now started with `node plugin/scripts/start-bridge.mjs` on both systems (plugin.json no longer runs a shell script). The launcher reads the Bitwarden token from the macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager, fetches only `OPENAI_REALTIME_API_KEY` with `bws secret list`, and runs the bridge in the same process, so the key still lives only in the bridge's memory and no long-running `bws` process holds it. Other secrets the machine account can read are no longer put in the bridge's environment.
- `npm run build:audio` is a Node script (`plugin/scripts/build-audio.mjs`) for both systems; `start-bridge.sh` and `build-audio.sh` are gone.
- `npm run setup` handles macOS and Windows: it checks for MSVC on Windows, stores the token in Credential Manager (typed without echo, twice), and checks that Bitwarden hands over the key the same way the launcher does. New `--new-token` replaces a stored token.
- On Windows, logs and the clip cache default to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\voice-wayfinder` when Claude Code does not set `CLAUDE_PLUGIN_DATA`.
- `npm test` quotes its glob with double quotes, so it also runs under cmd.exe.

## 0.2.0

- End means end: after the pane's End button (or `voice_control end`), every voice call answers `ended` without reconnecting or speaking, until voice mode is started again (`/voice-wayfinder:voice-mode`, which calls the new `voice_control start`). Before, Claude's next voice call reconnected and spoke. The pane says voice ended and how to start it again.
- Less room noise: a noise gate in the bridge learns the room's level and sends steady sound (fan, air conditioning, hum) as silence, so it no longer opens listening; a 120 ms lookahead keeps word starts whole (`audio.noiseGate`). macOS voice processing's automatic gain is off (`audio.autoGain`), so a quiet room is not turned up. Barge-in needs 500 ms of speech (was 250), so a throat clear no longer stops Claude. Coughs, "อืม", and stock phrases recognition invents from noise ("ขอบคุณที่รับชม") are dropped instead of taken as an answer. When macOS Voice Isolation is off, the pane suggests turning it on.
- Speaking while Claude works now gets an answer. What you say while Claude writes code or runs a build reaches it beside its next tool call's result (not only at its next voice question), and when Claude has finished its turn, what you say starts a new turn as your own words (marked 🎙). The skill tells Claude to answer at once by voice before carrying on. New `voice_control` action `take`, which the pane uses to collect those words once.
- Pane and captions restyled: one color per speaker (Claude orange, you blue) on every name; text in the normal color; the question card in an orange frame. The status animates while voice is on (sound bars while someone speaks, a breathing dot while listening, a spinner while connecting) in the speaker's color. Controls are three short buttons on one row (🔇 Mute · ⏭ Skip · ⏹ End). The conversation lists the newest line first.
- One-command setup: `npm run setup` checks the Mac, builds, stores the Bitwarden token in the Keychain, checks the OpenAI key arrives, installs the plugin and (after asking, with a backup) adds the voice tools allow rule to `~/.claude/settings.json`. `npm run setup:check` only reports. The README starts with a four-step quick start.
- The thinking cue is a soft harmonic tone made locally (a chord with a slow swell and shimmer), after 3 s and again every 8 s while Claude still thinks, instead of the same spoken words every time. `thinkingCue: "<text>"` still speaks a line instead; `""` turns it off.
- "ฟังไม่ทัน", "อีกที", "อะไรนะ", "repeat" and the like are no longer taken as the answer: the question is asked again, a little slower (up to 3 times), and the results say how often (`repeats`). Short utterances only, so a real answer that contains "again" still counts.
- The pane is now a live voice conversation, not a map: the question card Claude sends as `screen` (full question, numbered choices, recommendation; "Question 2 of 5" during a round), what Claude is saying, your words live as you speak, and the conversation. Answer by voice or by typing a choice.
- `voice_converse` and `voice_interview` take `screen` (Markdown for the card).
- Removed `voice_map_update` and the map from the pane, the state and the skill.
- `/voice-map` is now `/voice-pane`.
- `voice_status` is marked read-only, so auto mode lets the pane poll it. The pane explains a permission refusal and points at the allow rule in the README. It polls every 0.3 s while voice is on, every ~3 s while off.

## 0.1.0

- Realtime voice bridge (MCP server `voice`): `voice_say`, `voice_listen`, `voice_converse`, `voice_status`, `voice_map_update`, `voice_control`.
- OpenAI Realtime adapter (GA protocol, default model `gpt-realtime-2.1`) behind the `VoiceProvider` interface; Claude Code stays the brain, the realtime model never replies on its own.
- Native `vw-audio` helper: full-duplex 24 kHz PCM16, instant playback flush on barge-in, optional macOS voice processing (echo cancellation).
- Idle auto-disconnect, session refresh before the provider's 60-minute limit, running cost estimate, local JSONL transcripts with key redaction.
- `voice-mode` skill: spoken Thai rules for /wayfinder.
- Map pane mod (`/voice-map`): voice status, current ticket, decided / blocked / fog, live transcript, mute / skip / end buttons. Protocol v1.
- Secrets through Bitwarden Secrets Manager (`bws run`), access token in the macOS Keychain.

### Added after the first live tests

- Faster turns: speech at speed 1.2 with brisk-pace instructions; 0.5 s VAD silence and a 0.9 s end-of-reply window; per-turn timings in the log (first audio, transcription, Claude's gap).
- Instant acknowledgement ("ค่ะ") the moment a reply is complete and a thinking cue after 4 s, from clips rendered once and cached on disk.
- `voice_interview`: a whole grilling round asked back to back, with no wait on Claude between questions.
- Read along: Claude writes each spoken line on screen; the pane's Now box shows the current question and the words being heard live.
- Type to answer: Enter in the chat box during a voice question hands the text to the bridge as the answer (and stops Claude's speech); otherwise it goes to the chat as usual.
- Captions above the prompt (always in view, unlike a pane, which is held back in windows narrower than 144 columns unless the person opens it): status, Claude's current line, the live words or the last answer. A status line under the prompt shows voice state and cost. `/voice-wayfinder:voice-mode` opens the map pane as the person's own request, so it is placed at any width.
- Cost summary at every voice session end (duration, speech and transcription), plus today's and this month's totals from the local logs, in the transcript, the pane and `voice_status`. Log folders are named by local date.

### Fixed during the first live test

- The launcher quotes paths for `bws run`, which joins its arguments into one shell string (the plugin folder has a space).
- No vocabulary word list in the transcription `prompt`: on noise, the recogniser returned the list as a fake utterance. Vocabulary now goes only to models with a `keywords` field.
- Noise no longer cuts Claude off: the mic stays closed for 0.6 s after the audio engine starts, barge-in needs 250 ms of sustained speech (`bargeInConfirmMs`), utterances under 300 ms are dropped (`minUtteranceMs`), and the VAD threshold is 0.6.
- Echo cancellation: the playback chain is reconnected after voice processing reconfigures the engine (nothing played before).
